Ke Wang

Applied Scientist at Amazon

Research Interests: LLM Post-Training, Nonlinear Optimization, AI for Science

About me: I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon, where I work on post-training for large language and vision-language models, focusing on preference alignment, long-context understanding, personalization, and efficient reasoning, across dense and MoE models from 4B to 100B+ parameters. I drive model development end to end, from data curation and training to evaluation and production deployment. I received my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Alexander W. Dowling. My PhD research combined operations research and machine learning, using active learning to solve nonlinear optimization problems and accelerate materials discovery.
